# Privacy Policy for MyValidCV

Last updated: 20 August 2026

MyValidCV is an online service based in the United Kingdom. Contact: support@myvalidcv.com.

## Information we collect

We process account details, contact information, CVs and job descriptions submitted for analysis, applicant information uploaded by authorised enterprise customers, analysis results, subscription and payment records, support and feedback messages, consent records, and limited technical/security information. Google or LinkedIn sign-in provides only the identity information and permissions approved by the user. MyValidCV does not receive the user's social-account password.

## Why we use information

We use personal information to provide accounts and requested services, compare CVs with job descriptions, produce candidate-support or enterprise screening reports, administer subscriptions and payments, prevent abuse, secure and improve the service, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations. Depending on the activity, the lawful basis is performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, consent for optional analytics or marketing, or compliance with law.

## Recruitment and automated analysis

MyValidCV provides decision-support guidance. Scores and recommendations do not guarantee employment and must not be treated as the sole hiring decision. Enterprise customers are responsible for lawful candidate notices, their hiring decisions, and human review. The platform is designed not to rank candidates by age or protected characteristics. Users may ask the relevant enterprise employer to explain or review a hiring decision.

## Sharing and processors

Information may be processed by providers needed for hosting, database storage, email, payments, authentication, analytics where consented, security, and technical support. Enterprise applicant information is available to the authorised enterprise account that uploaded it. We do not sell personal information. We disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

## International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections should be used. Contact us for information about applicable safeguards.

## Retention

Uploaded individual and enterprise candidate CV records are configured for deletion after 30 days unless a shorter period is required or the service clearly states otherwise. Account, consent, transaction, security, and audit records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for their stated purpose, contractual requirements, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Anonymised or aggregated statistics may be retained longer where they no longer identify an individual.

## Security

We use access controls, permission separation, audit records, encrypted HTTPS connections, restricted administrative functions, and other proportionate safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should not upload information that is unnecessary for the requested analysis.

## Analytics and communications

Optional analytics run only after consent and can be withdrawn in Account settings. Essential cookies remain necessary for login, security, and core service operation. Marketing communications require an applicable lawful basis and can be opted out of.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, and withdraw consent. The right to object to processing based on legitimate interests can be exercised by contacting support@myvalidcv.com. We may need to verify identity before completing a request.

## Complaints and contact

Contact support@myvalidcv.com first so we can investigate. Individuals may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

## Changes

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.
