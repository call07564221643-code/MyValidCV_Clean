Quality before size

Controlled activation

How approval works

  1. Apply. Tell us who you are, where your audience is and how you intend to promote.
  2. Owner review. We assess relevance (30%), quality (20%), engagement (20%), compliance (20%) and audience size (10%).
  3. Online meeting. We confirm audience fit, approved channels, disclosures, brand rules and realistic expectations.
  4. Decision and agreement. Approval may include conditions. You then accept the current agreement and privacy terms.
  5. Activation. The owner creates and activates your unique referral code; approval alone does not activate it.
  6. Promote and earn. Valid conversions are reported, held for refunds and paid after the threshold is met.

What we ask for

  • Legal name, country and applicant type
  • Website and public social profiles
  • Audience size, geography and engagement evidence
  • Audience description and planned channels
  • Promotion plan and relevant experience
  • Confirmation of genuine audience and compliant advertising

Non-negotiable rules

  • Clearly label affiliate advertising
  • No spam, fake engagement or misleading claims
  • No guaranteed job, interview or ATS outcome claims
  • No bidding on protected brand terms without permission
  • No self-referrals, cookie manipulation or unauthorised discounts
  • Respect privacy and applicable marketing law

Before you apply

You do not need a large following. You do need a relevant, authentic audience and a credible plan. Commission applies only to eligible, successfully paid and retained transactions. Refunds, chargebacks, fraud and cancelled payments can reverse commission.

Programme approval is discretionary. Commercial terms may be confirmed in your agreement. This page is an operational summary and does not replace the signed agreement or applicable law.
